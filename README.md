### Preprint

**Gene–metabolite networks reveal physiological trade-offs but not transcriptional co-regulation between carotenoid and dry matter accumulation in cassava (Manihot esculenta) roots"** (Villwock et al. _preprint_)

### Raw data
Raw RNA sequencing data is publicly available at NCBI Sequencing Read Archive (SRA) under BioProject ID PRJNA1258369 (http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/1258369). 

### Germplasm 
Cassava accessions used in this study were contributed to the National Clonal Germplasm Repository at the USDA Subtropical Horticulture Research Station, Miami, Florida.
